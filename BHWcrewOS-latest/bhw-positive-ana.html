BHW Medical Group · analysis · patterns · management
⚕️ Clinical decision support, not a diagnosis. Maps ANA titer + pattern + antibody panels + symptoms against the BHW Autoimmune Master Matrix (100 conditions) to organize a differential and surface confirmatory testing. Always correlate clinically; the chart is the source of truth.
Central rule: a patient can have excessive autoimmune effector activity and inadequate antimicrobial competence at the same time. The goal is never to boost or suppress the whole immune system — it is to identify which circuit needs calming and which needs support. Patterns are not diagnoses; organ-threatening disease, active infection, thrombosis, bleeding, and respiratory or neurologic instability override all elective pattern work.
