Microvascular dysfunction is missed on standard cath. INOCA, MINOCA, exertional dyspnea, post-viral fatigue, brain fog with normal MRI — many of these share a common driver: the small vessels that actually move oxygen.

Flow is BHW Medical's vascular line of work — built to find, measure, and stabilize what gets called "anxiety" by everyone else.

02Who Flow is for

Symptoms that point to vascular, even when imaging looks "normal."

Cardiac

INOCA · MINOCA

Angina with non-obstructive coronaries. Clean cath but persistent chest pain on exertion.

Cardiac

Exertional dyspnea

Breathlessness that exceeds what your imaging suggests. HFpEF workup welcome.

Cerebral

Brain fog · low blood flow

Cognitive fatigue, lightheadedness on standing, post-meal slumps.

Autonomic

POTS · orthostatic intolerance

Tilt-table candidates, dysautonomia, exercise intolerance.

Post-viral

Long COVID vascular pattern

Endothelial injury, microclots, exercise intolerance with normal cardiac echo.

Metabolic

Endothelial dysfunction

Pre-clinical vascular disease in the setting of insulin resistance or metabolic syndrome.

03The Flow protocol

A four-phase workup, designed for depth of signal.

01

Vascular history

60-minute structured intake. Timeline of every "you're fine" workup so far.

02

Functional testing

Tilt, EndoPAT, NIRS, hs-CRP, lipoprotein subfractions, ApoB. The full microvascular picture.

03

Mechanism map

Inflammatory, autonomic, metabolic, or hemodynamic — we name the driver before treating.

04

Stabilization plan

Medication where indicated. Pacing, hydration, salt, sleep, supplementation — measured every 12 weeks.

Coordination

Flow is operated inside BHW Medical. Your primary care, behavioral, cognitive, and nutrition modules share one record. Referrals don't restart your story.
