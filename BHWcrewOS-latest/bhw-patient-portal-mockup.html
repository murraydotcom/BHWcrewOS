Your labs, explained
Ferritin18 ng/mL · low

Ferritin is your body's iron storage. Yours is running low — which matters because iron is how your blood carries oxygen to every tissue, including your brain. Low storage often shows up exactly the way you described it: afternoon fatigue, foggy focus, feeling winded on stairs.

Vitamin D24 ng/mL · insufficient

Vitamin D acts more like a hormone than a vitamin — it's involved in mood, immune function, and how your muscles recover.

The physiology

When iron storage is low, your cells make energy less efficiently — think of it as your engine running on fewer cylinders. Your body compensates by borrowing from stress hormones, which is why the crash comes in the afternoon when that borrowed energy runs out. This week's plan is built to stop the borrowing and start the rebuilding.

How your lifestyle connects

Coffee before food blocks iron absorption — that's why the protein anchor comes first. The post-meal walks blunt the glucose spike that deepens the 3pm dip. And the sleep window is when the actual rebuilding happens; iron repletion without sleep is pouring water into a cracked cup.

Your supplements
Iron bisglycinate · 25 mg
Every other morning, with vitamin C (or orange slices), away from coffee by 1 hour.
Why: gentler on your stomach than iron sulfate; every-other-day dosing actually absorbs better.
Vitamin D3 · 2,000 IU
Daily, with your largest meal — it needs fat to absorb.
Why: rebuilding to the 40–60 range where energy and mood support live.
