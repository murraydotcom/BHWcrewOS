What this is

CMS Data at the Point of Care

DPC returns Medicare claims for your attributed patients — including each patient's last Annual Wellness Visit (from HCPCS G0438/G0439), which eligibility checks can't provide. Sign in with your crewOS PIN, install your credentials once (step 1), then verify the connection (step 2). Your private key is stored server-side in Netlify Blobs — it's too large for Netlify's function environment limit — so it never lives in an env var or in the page.

1Install credentials — one time, stored securely

Paste your DPC private key (PEM) here while signed in. It's saved to Netlify Blobs on the server — never rendered back, never committed to git. Client token, key id, base, and group id are optional here (they can also stay as DPC_* env vars); anything you set here overrides the env var.

4Last AWV + gaps in care per patient

    
Reads each patient's last Annual Wellness Visit and their open care gaps — preventive/quality services (screenings, vaccines, diabetes measures) with no recent Medicare claim. Claims-based screening aid; always confirm in the chart.
