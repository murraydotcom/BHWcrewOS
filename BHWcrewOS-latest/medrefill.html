Medication Refill Desk

FrontDeskOS · Patient Requests
← Front Desk
Workflow boundary: this page records and tracks a refill request. It does not create, alter, transmit, or approve a prescription. CharmHealth remains the prescribing record; an authorized prescriber makes every clinical decision and signs every prescription.

1. Find the patient

Search the protected Patient Registry first. The refill request cannot be saved without an exact, active BHW patient record.

2. Record the request

Use the exact active medication and pharmacy shown in CharmHealth. The prescription details, including SIG, stay in the existing prescription record - do not re-enter or change them here.

Established continuation: verify the prior prescription, pharmacy, allergies, last completed visit, monitoring, and any hold before routing for prescriber signature.

3. Screening or patient follow-up

Choose the clinician-directed follow-up needed for this request. Selecting an item creates a visible next action in the Patient Request; it does not send a questionnaire automatically.

4. Create the request

Saving creates one patient-linked Medication request in BHW Cloud, visible in FrontDeskOS and Patient Requests. It begins as Received; no automatic patient message is sent.

Where it saves

One operational request, linked to the Registry patient ID:

  • FrontDeskOS - Received
  • Patient Requests - status history, owner, and follow-through
  • Patient Registry - same BHW patient link
  • myBHW - patient-facing questionnaire only after staff selects an approved instrument
  • CharmHealth - prescription preparation, signature, and e-Rx status

Immediate holds

Do not treat the queue as a clinical decision tool. Route urgently for potentially harmful interruption, serious adverse effects, a medication discrepancy, high-risk behavioral-health concern, pregnancy-related change, dehydration/vomiting, abnormal sugar symptoms, or an emergency.

Questionnaire status

The Primary Care AWV questionnaire contains preliminary alcohol, recreational-drug, nonmedical-prescription, and opioid-use questions; it flags “Substance-use follow-up.” The earlier Mind & Mood Recovery Readiness Check is only a mockup. Neither is an approved standalone substance-use follow-up instrument for this page, so no questionnaire is sent automatically.
