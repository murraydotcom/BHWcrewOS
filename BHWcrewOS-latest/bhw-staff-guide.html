01Signing in & your home page

How to get in

  1. Go to bhwcrewos.netlify.app on any phone, tablet, or computer.
  2. Pick your name from the list, enter your PIN, tap Sign in.
  3. You land on your home page — front desk for Shadé, Chronic Care for the care coordinator, Primary Care for Amaris and Yahaira, and so on.
Everything else is one tap away. The list down the left side (or across the top on a phone) is the rail — every page you're allowed to see lives there. You always land on your own page; you're never locked out of the others.
Never share your PIN. Every action you take is stamped with your name. If you think someone else knows it, tell Amaris and it gets changed.

03The daily rhythm

Front desk day Shadé + coverage

  1. 9:00 — sweep the shared inbox. Open info@bhwmedical.org and work the labels top to bottom: 📞 voicemails, 📠 faxes, 💬 portal messages.
  2. Log each one into the Triage Queue — patient name, callback number, what they need, priority.
  3. Work the Front Desk page by color. Red first, then orange, then yellow. Green can wait.
  4. Sweep again mid-afternoon (about 3:00) so nothing sits overnight.
  5. Before you leave: every red and orange item is either done or has a first response logged.
Texts arrive by themselves. Once the phone system is connected, patient texts create their own queue entries — you'll see them appear without logging anything.

What the colors mean

The clock stops when you tap Start — not when you finish. If you've reached out and are waiting on the patient or a specialist, Start it so it stops screaming red while you wait.

04Front Desk OS — the four things it does

1 · Today's inbox Front desk

Three columns — calls & voicemails, texts & portal, faxes & other. Each card has buttons:

  • Start — you're on it. Stops the SLA clock and stamps your first response.
  • ✓ Done — handled and closed.
  • 💬 Reply — text the patient back from the main line. Pick a canned response or type your own.
The canned responses cover Rx sent, refill received, appointment confirmed, we'll call you, records sent, and running behind. Pick one, edit if needed, send.

2 · Patient lookup Everyone

Type a phone number or name → their profile appears before you answer the call.

  • Left side: allergies (red box at the top), phone, insurance, member #, last and next visit, medications.
  • Right side: everything they've contacted us about, newest first — so you know if there's an open urgent item on their account.
The clinical snapshot is for context and routing only. Use it to decide "she's on a blood thinner — flag this refill as high." Never read a medication list back to a patient as final, and never make a clinical decision from it. CharmHealth is the chart. Check the "as of" date at the bottom — if it's old, verify in Charm.

3 · Referral finder Front desk, providers

  1. Choose the specialty and the patient's insurance.
  2. Tap Find in-network — preferred partners (⭐) come first.
  3. Copy fax grabs their fax number, or → Use in referral loads them straight into the referral form.

4 · Generate a referral Front desk, providers

  1. Look the patient up first, then tap use last lookup — name, DOB, phone, member #, and insurance fill in.
  2. Pick the specialist from the dropdown (or use the finder's button) — their whole block fills, including the fax.
  3. Provider fills in reason, brief history, what's being requested, place of service, number of visits.
  4. Tap Generate referral PDF — it downloads and opens ready to print or fax.
Always verify the member # against the card before it goes out. A wrong member number is the #1 reason referrals bounce back.

05Patient pages — setting a patient up

Giving a patient their page Providers, care coordination

  1. Open the Patient Pages Hub in Notion and duplicate the 🌿 template.
  2. Rename it to the patient's first name and fill in their sections at visit close.
  3. Tap Share → Invite, enter the patient's personal email, set permission to Can comment, send.
  4. The patient gets an email invitation, creates a free account with that same email, and sees only their page.
  5. Paste the page link into their row on the Patients Master List so everyone can find it.
  6. Text or email them the welcome message (it's saved on the Hub page — copy and paste).
Never put a linked database view on a patient page. It would expose other patients. Patient pages hold plain content only — text, tables, and their own updates.
If they can't get in: check spam for the Notion invite, and make sure they signed up with the exact email you invited. A different email means no access — re-invite the one they actually use.

06BHWCrewCare Portal — the provider side

Publishing a week of the care plan Providers

  1. Open BHWCrewCare Portal → Care plan builder.
  2. Pick the patient, tap Load their care plan — their existing plan appears on the left.
  3. Write that week: number, title, what they're focusing on, and a note in your own voice.
  4. Choose your name in Signed, tap Publish week to patient page.

Posting a lab result

  1. Post results tab → pick the patient → enter the lab and value.
  2. Write what it means for them in plain language.
  3. Tap Post to patient page.
A patient never sees a raw number alone. The system won't post without your interpretation — that's on purpose.

07Care coordination requests

Asking care coordination for help Any division

On the Chronic Care page, the "Request care coordination" card has a button for each reason:

  • SDOH resources — housing, food, transportation, utilities, benefits
  • Acute follow-up — what happened, what was done, and by when
  • Chronic follow-up — which condition and the interval needed
  • Managing multiple specialists — what's falling through the cracks
  • Care plan hard to manage — which part, and the barrier the patient named
  • Needs more time between visits — what they're asking for and why
  • Monitoring equipment — which device and the clinical reason
Each button opens a referral already addressed to Chronic Care with the type filled in. Add the Needed by timeframe so the coordinator knows the urgency.

10Rules that never bend

Safety and privacy

  • Crisis: if a patient is in danger or talking about harming themselves — 988 (call or text) or 911. Do not leave it in a queue. Tell a provider immediately.
  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke symptoms, heavy bleeding: 911 first, then tell a provider.
  • Never text or email PHI to a personal phone or personal email — yours or anyone's.
  • Patient information stays in the systems. No screenshots, no photos of screens, no forwarding to personal accounts.
  • Verify in Charm before acting on anything clinical you read on a dashboard.
  • Log it, don't remember it. If a patient asked for something, it goes in the queue — even if you're about to handle it.
