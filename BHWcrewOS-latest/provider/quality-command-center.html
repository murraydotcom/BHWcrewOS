Quality-system boundary

Health Core owns clinical truth and provenance. The Quality Command Center reads verified evidence and calculates operational status. CrewOS owns outreach/follow-through. CMS/QPP owns official eligibility, scoring, attribution, registration, and payment adjustments. No external record automatically becomes clinical truth without the reconciliation rules of its owning Health Core domain.
Non-PHI planning fields on this page may be stored in this browser. Do not enter patient names, dates of birth, member IDs, or other PHI in the planning inputs. This Command Center never registers an MVP, elects MIPS participation, or submits data to CMS.
