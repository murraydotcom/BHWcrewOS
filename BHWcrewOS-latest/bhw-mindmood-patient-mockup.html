02Our promise — three commitments

A clinic where your physiology and your story sit at the same table.

i.

Physiology aware

Mood symptoms with normal MRI still have biology. We coordinate with primary care, Flow, and Nutrition findings — not in isolation from them.

ii.

Never punitive

No "earning back" privileges, no shame protocols. Relapse is information, not failure. The frame is repair, not punishment.

iii.

Built to last

Stabilize the acute thing, then build the structure: meaning, community, work, family — the things mood medication alone can't reach.

03What we treat

For mood, trauma, and substance recovery — often all at once.

d

Treatment-resistant depression

Patients who've cycled through SSRIs without traction. Workup includes thyroid, inflammation, methylation, sleep architecture.

a

Anxiety + autonomic dysregulation

Co-managed with Flow when vascular and orthostatic findings show up. Body symptoms taken seriously.

t

Trauma + complex PTSD

EMDR, somatic, IFS-informed. Pacing matters more than protocol — we don't rush stabilization.

s

Substance use · alcohol · stimulants

Outpatient, medication-assisted when indicated, harm-reduction first. No abstinence-only gatekeeping.

b

Bipolar spectrum

Bipolar II often miscoded as treatment-resistant depression. We screen carefully and coordinate psychiatry from day one.

p

Perinatal mood

Postpartum depression, anxiety, intrusive thoughts. Family-system aware. Lactation-safe pharmacology.
